Project name: Quantum Jukebox

Create a full visual identity pack for a futuristic Discord music bot inspired by Star Citizen.

Tone and vibe:
- premium, high-end, sci-fi, clean, tactical UI, aerospace engineering precision
- not cartoonish, not childish, no meme style
- sharp, modern, cinematic, trustworthy

Core concept:
- "Quantum Jukebox" is a modular music bot that travels through playlists like a ship traveling through quantum lanes
- mix starship navigation aesthetics with advanced audio technology
- visual metaphors: quantum route lines, holographic waveform rings, cockpit HUD overlays, orbital tracks, stellar map grids

Color direction:
- deep navy (#0A1020), carbon black (#0E0E12), steel gray (#4C566A)
- accent cyan (#39D7FF), electric teal (#16F2C4), warm amber highlights (#F9B24B)
- use subtle gradients and glow, avoid neon overload

Typography style:
- geometric sci-fi sans
- uppercase labels for UI elements
- compact technical microtext feel

Main mascot/symbol idea:
- a sleek starship-inspired audio core icon (fusion of music wave + quantum drive ring)
- no human character
- logo should work both detailed and minimal

Deliverables to generate:
1) Primary logo (transparent background)
2) Alternate icon/avatar (square, Discord-ready)
3) Wide hero banner for a bot website
4) Discord server banner (center-safe composition)
5) Minimal monochrome mark for watermark/favicon
6) 3 background variants (dark HUD, star map, soft gradient with orbital lines)

Composition guidance:
- keep strong focal point centered for avatar/icon
- for banners, keep text-safe area uncluttered in the middle
- include layered depth: foreground HUD, mid waveform ring, distant stars
- imply motion and speed (quantum jump effect) without chaos

Optional text for mockups:
- "Quantum Jukebox"
- "High-End Modular Music Bot"
- "Playlist Navigation. Reimagined."

Rendering style:
- ultra detailed digital illustration with crisp edges
- realistic glow, volumetric light, subtle film grain
- high contrast but readable
- polished production-grade branding concept art

Negative constraints:
- no fantasy swords, no medieval motifs, no anime faces
- no generic equalizer bars only; integrate audio motif into spacecraft language
- avoid purple-dominant palettes
